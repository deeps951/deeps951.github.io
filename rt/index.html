About Me

Hi there! I’m Andrew, a creative soul based on the beautiful Gold Coast in Queensland, Australia. By day, I work as a Public Area Cleaner in a residential tower, but my true passions lie in music, photography, and design.

I’m a multi-instrumentalist who plays bass guitar and saxophone, and I love pouring my emotions into songwriting. When I’m not making music, you’ll find me behind the lens capturing life’s beauty, or designing with a unique human-centered approach—using colors inspired by the body, like hair, eyes, and skin tones, to create designs that feel alive.

Whether I’m crafting melodies, exploring exciting places locally or abroad, or enjoying a perfectly brewed coffee or a creative cocktail, I aim to live life with style and passion. Welcome to my portfolio—let’s create something amazing together!
