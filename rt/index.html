About Me

Andrew is a designer, musician, and storyteller based on the Gold Coast, blending earthy human elements with digital craftsmanship to create experiences that are both soulful and striking. Whether he’s capturing fleeting light through a lens, composing beats that pulse with emotion, or building beautifully structured web pages, his work reflects a deep reverence for rhythm — in sound, in shape, in story. With an instinct for harmony and a passion for authenticity, Andrew doesn’t just make things — he makes meaning.

A lifelong learner and creator, Andrew moves fluidly between code and creativity, sipping coffee in the morning and crafting synth lines by night. He draws inspiration from the textures of nature — the rhythm of waves, the stillness of dusk, the quiet wisdom in tangled branches and coastal skies. With a curious heart and an intentional hand, he invites you into a world where art meets function, elegance meets edge, and every click or chord is an invitation to feel something real.
