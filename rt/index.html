Hi, I'm Andrew 👋

Welcome to my little corner of the web. I’m a passionate web developer and music maker based on the sunny Gold Coast in Queensland, Australia. My work blends creativity, precision, and a deep love for human connection — whether I’m writing clean code, composing melodic techno, or capturing a moment through the lens of my camera.

Before diving into the world of web and music full-time, I was a flying instructor — teaching aerobatics and flight fundamentals. That experience gave me a calm, focused approach I now bring into every project I take on. Today, I craft accessible websites with a designer’s eye, drawing from natural color palettes and bold accents to create something truly human. Whether you’re here for my music, my photography, or to geek out over HTML and CSS, I’m glad you stopped by.

My Experience

Web Developer: I create responsive, human-centered websites using modern technologies like HTML5, CSS3, Tailwind CSS, and Bootstrap. I love designing interfaces that feel natural and intuitive — using code as a creative tool to shape experiences that connect with people. I approach development with a designer’s mindset, paying attention to structure, flow, and the emotional tone that a digital space can evoke.

Musician: Music has always been part of my rhythm. I play bass guitar and saxophone, and I write and produce my own tracks — often exploring melodic techno and emotionally rich soundscapes in Logic Pro. For me, music is both expressive and grounding; it’s where I find flow, emotion, and freedom. It also fuels the way I think about timing, harmony, and rhythm in other creative disciplines — like coding and visual design.

Former Flying Instructor: In a previous chapter of my life, I taught people how to fly — including aerobatics. It was an incredible experience that taught me clarity, patience, and how to stay grounded under pressure (pun intended!). While I’ve since moved on, the lessons I learned in the cockpit still shape my approach to life and work: stay focused, stay calm, and enjoy the journey.
