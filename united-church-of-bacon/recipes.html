✦   THE FOUR FEASTS

Let there be breakfast.

Four simple dishes for pilgrims of every skill level. These are actual recipes, not merely theatrical props.

RECIPE 01

The Sacred Skillet

A classic breakfast bacon ritual

◷   15–20 min♧   Serves 2
Open the sacred method ＋

What thou shalt gather

  • 6–8 slices of bacon
  • A sturdy frying pan
  • Kitchen paper for draining

The sacred ritual

  1. Lay the bacon in a cool, dry pan without overlapping.
  2. Cook over medium to medium-low heat, turning occasionally, until thoroughly cooked and as crisp as you like.
  3. Transfer to a paper-lined plate and rest briefly before serving.

✦ A low and steady flame is more virtuous than a burnt offering.

RECIPE 02

The Cathedral BLT

A sandwich worthy of stained glass

◷   15 min♧   Serves 2
Open the sacred method ＋

What thou shalt gather

  • 6 slices cooked bacon
  • 4 slices toasted sourdough
  • 2 lettuce leaves
  • 1 ripe tomato, sliced
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise

The sacred ritual

  1. Toast the bread and spread it lightly with mayonnaise.
  2. Layer lettuce, tomato and thoroughly cooked bacon onto two slices.
  3. Top with the remaining bread; cut diagonally with ceremonial dignity.

✦ The triangle is not a theological requirement. We are simply fond of it.

RECIPE 03

The Sunday Benediction

Maple-kissed bacon for the devoted

◷   20–25 min♧   Serves 3
Open the sacred method ＋

What thou shalt gather

  • 8 slices thick-cut bacon
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  • A little cracked black pepper

The sacred ritual

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan) and line a tray with baking paper.
  2. Arrange the bacon in a single layer, brush lightly with syrup and add pepper.
  3. Bake until thoroughly cooked and caramelised, watching carefully near the end; the syrup can burn quickly.

✦ Allow the hot glaze to cool briefly before handling.

RECIPE 04

Pilgrim’s Breakfast Hash

The holy union of potato and pan

◷   30–35 min♧   Serves 3
Open the sacred method ＋

What thou shalt gather

  • 3 medium potatoes, diced small
  • 4 rashers of cooked bacon, chopped
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt, pepper and fresh parsley

The sacred ritual

  1. Parboil the diced potatoes until just tender, then drain well.
  2. Warm the oil in a large pan; cook onion and potatoes until golden and cooked through.
  3. Stir in the fully cooked bacon and parsley; season and serve hot.

✦ A hearty reward for anyone who survived morning service without coffee.

PRACTICAL WISDOM FROM THE KITCHEN

Faith is no substitute for food safety.

Cook pork thoroughly; wash your hands and utensils after handling raw meat; and never leave cooking fat unattended. If a pan of oil or fat catches fire, turn off the heat if safe, cover it with a fire blanket or suitable lid, and never use water. When in doubt, call emergency services.
