✦ CHAPTER IV · THE CONFESSIONAL

Forgive me,
for I have sizzled.

All kitchens have secrets. Some are merely embarrassing. Others involve a microwave. Open these fictional confessions and discover the prescribed penance.

✦   SEALED TESTIMONIES

Behind the curtain.

These are invented confessions for entertainment. No real confessions are accepted, submitted or stored on this static website.

CASE FILE 001  ·  CONFIDENTIAL(ISH)THE MICROWAVE INCIDENTLift the velvet curtain ＋

“I reheated bacon in the microwave, and it emerged with the texture of a damp envelope.”

THE PENANCE: Your penance: make one proper breakfast on the stove. Speak kindly to the microwave; it was not at fault.

ABSOLUTION GRANTED · WITH CONDITIONS

“I told everyone the bacon was finished, then ate the final slice in secret.”

THE PENANCE: Your penance: offer someone the first slice next time. Do not call it a donation.

ABSOLUTION GRANTED · WITH CONDITIONS
