✦ CHAPTER I · THE SACRED LAW

The Ten
Commandments.

Carved into imaginary stone. Preserved for posterity. Occasionally revised after brunch.

✦   THE ARTICLES OF BREAKFAST

Hear, and obey.

Ten decrees from the Order of the Golden Skillet. The legal department insists these are not legally binding.

I

Thou shalt honour the crunch.

There is a sacred difference between crisp and charcoal. Thou shalt learn it.

II

Thou shalt not leave the skillet unattended.

A watchful eye is worth a hundred smoke alarms and one very awkward explanation.

III

Thou shalt season thy pan.

A well-loved skillet is a vessel of peace, flavour and mild domestic superiority.

IV

Thou shalt share the final slice.

Unless all parties have reached a legally binding agreement beforehand.

V

Thou shalt not confuse bacon with a bookmark.

The librarians have asked us to make this one absolutely explicit.

VI

Thou shalt respect the humble BLT.

Lettuce, tomato, bread and bacon: four pillars holding civilisation together.

VII

Thou shalt never rush the breakfast.

The microwave is fast. So is making an appalling decision.

VIII

Thou shalt honour thy fellow diners.

Whether crispy, chewy or somewhere in between, let no breakfast feud divide the table.

IX

Thou shalt keep a clean kitchen.

For grease on the floor is not a miracle. It is a slipping hazard.

X

Thou shalt never waste a glorious slice.

Take only what thou canst enjoy, and return for seconds with gratitude.

THE MOST IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION

“Thou shalt not be a jerk about breakfast.”

Good food should bring people together. Our congregation celebrates bacon, welcomes every appetite and encourages generous hospitality.
